# SYNAPSE: Weaver of Reality

A unique, kinetic physics-platformer built with HTML5, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.

## 🌌 The Concept
In the dark void of the Mindscape, there is no floor. You are a **Neural Impulse** that moves by firing glowing **Synapses** at floating neurons. 

Unlike other games, your tethers are **persistent**—they stay in the world, allowing you to weave complex webs, swing through massive gaps, or build "sky-bridges" to walk on. 

## 🚀 Features
- **Infinite Procedural World**: Travel infinitely as the world generates around you.
- **Kinetic Movement**: Swing, Dash, and Walk on your own history.
- **Juicy Feedback**: Screen shake, particle explosions, and a dynamic combo system.
- **Premium Aesthetic**: Modern Glasmorphism UI with a neon cyber-organic theme.

## 🎮 How to Play
1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.
2. **W/A/S/D** to move.
3. **Left-Click & Hold** on nodes to fire tethers.
4. **Right-Click** towards your mouse to **Dash**.
5. **Space** to dissolve your oldest threads and clear the path.

## 🛠️ Technology
- **Core**: HTML5 Canvas
- **Physics**: Custom-built verlet-lite physics engine.
- **Style**: Modern Vanilla CSS.
